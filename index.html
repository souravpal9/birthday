welcome to 21 ✨


21 years. still becoming.

i thought of earrings. then a letter. then this.

i wanted to give you something. then i realised— sometimes the difficult part isn’t choosing a gift. it’s figuring out how to give it.

so i made this instead. something you don’t have to carry. something you don’t have to hide. something that can just exist.


and there’s one more thing i wanted to say.

thank you.

not in the dramatic way. not because birthdays make people emotional. just genuinely— thank you for existing in my life.

for the random conversations. for the moments that probably meant nothing to you but stayed with me. for unknowingly becoming part of memories i’ll remember for a long time.

i don’t think people always realise the effect they have on others. sometimes they just show up, be themselves, and quietly leave things a little better than before.

you did that.

and maybe life changes. people get busy. paths become different. things become memories.

but even then— i’m grateful that our timelines crossed at all.

thank you for being part of my story. and thank you for giving me memories that made making this feel worth it.


this page will end. the butterflies will disappear. the music will stop. but i hope your year starts beautifully.

happy birthday again.
welcome to 21.


— thank you for staying —
